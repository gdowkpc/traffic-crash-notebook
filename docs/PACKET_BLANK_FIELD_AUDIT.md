# Investigation packet blank-field audit

Scope: the Full Working Packet and Compact Completed-Case Packet generated by
`pdf_export.py`. Quick Review uses the same blank-value formatter and was checked
as well. The separate Traffic Crash Exchange Report has its own renderer.

| Packet area | Blank-value paths audited | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cover | Crash date/time, investigator, DPSST, assignment, location, and routing identifiers | Leave the value space empty while preserving the table cell height. |
| Investigative packet | Checklist dates, DA and court fields, charges, crash details, scene evidence, and video sources | Remove the shared dash placeholder. |
| Road, weather, and hit-and-run | Conditions, roadway and surface observations, evidence, vehicle leads, and person leads | Remove blank field dashes and omit heading separators when a component is blank. |
| People and participants | Roles, identity, address, contact, occupation, injury, transport, restraint, and driver details | Remove blank field dashes and the separate blank identity fallback. |
| Vehicles | Identification, plate, VIN, driver, owner, insurance, towing, release, inspections, tires, and motorcycle items | Remove blank field and missing-person dashes; omit a leading separator when the vehicle number is blank. |
| Witnesses and VRU analysis | Witness and contact details, linked person/vehicle labels, and analysis fields | Remove blank field and missing-link dashes; omit the slash when a linked vehicle is absent. |
| Evidence, tasks, and journal | Receipt and item values, task values, journal date/time/category/details | Remove the shared dash placeholder. |

Blank means `None`, an empty string, or whitespace-only text. An invisible space
keeps a printable value row at its existing height, so handwriting does not have
to cross out a mark. Entered values, including any dash entered by the user,
remain unchanged. Dashes that separate two present values and fixed report
wording remain.

Verification: `tests/test_pdf_export.py` exports a sparse case in Full, Compact,
and Quick Review modes and checks their extracted text for standalone dash
placeholders and orphaned separators. Rendered page review checks that blank
cells retain usable space.
